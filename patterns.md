# buildgreen.ai patterns

## Visual posture
- Editorial, open-source, living-document aesthetic
- Warm paper background with deep forest ink and one restrained green accent
- Serif display moments paired with technical sans body copy
- No gradients, glassmorphism, dashboard-card spam, or generic SaaS hero layouts

## Layout system
- 12-column page grid with aligned section edges
- Full-width hero composition with oversized typographic anchor
- Sticky left rail for principle navigation and scroll progress
- Principle sections use anchored IDs for citation and linking

## Motion system
- Hero reveal sequence only once on load
- Sticky progress rail updates with scroll position
- Tactile press states on buttons and citation controls
- Hover motion stays crisp and low-amplitude
- Reduced-motion mode disables transforms/transitions and preserves clarity

## Components
- Citation chip/button with copy-link feedback
- Principle blocks with oversized numeric markers and quiet separators
- Case-study metric rows driven by real numbers only
- Roadmap timeline with restrained connector lines
